# Privacy Policy

**App:** MarketDataApp (working title)
**Developer:** Hemanth (independent developer)
**Contact:** hemanthdvng1@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

MarketDataApp is an Android tool that lets you download NSE market data through
your own Zerodha Kite Connect account, view live quotes, scan charts for
patterns, and chat with an AI assistant using your own AI API keys.

The developer does not run any server for this app and does not collect,
receive, or store your data. Everything described below stays on your device or
goes directly from your device to the third-party service you chose to connect.

## Data stored on your device
- Your Kite Connect API key, API secret and session access token, and your
  Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) API keys. These are kept in Android
  encrypted storage and are never sent to the developer.
- Instrument lists, download history and scanner settings (local database).
- CSV files you download, saved to the folder you pick.
- App backup is disabled, so this data is not copied to cloud backups.

## Data sent to third parties (directly from your device)
- **Zerodha Kite Connect (kite.zerodha.com, api.kite.trade):** your login via
  Zerodha's own page, and requests for market data. Governed by Zerodha's
  privacy policy.
- **Anthropic (Claude) and/or Google (Gemini):** only when you use the AI Agent
  tab. Your messages, any file you attach, and the market data shown in the
  app (quotes, option chain, CSV summaries) are sent to the provider you
  select, using your API key. Governed by that provider's privacy policy.

## What we do not do
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data by the developer.

## Permissions
- Internet: to reach the services above.
- Notifications: to alert you about pattern signals and show the scanner status.
- Foreground service: to run the optional background pattern scanner while you
  have it switched on. You can stop it any time from the notification.

## Your control
Use Logout in the app to remove your session token. Clear app data or uninstall
to remove everything stored on your device.

## Not financial advice
Market data, pattern signals and AI responses are for information and education
only. They are not investment advice, may be inaccurate or delayed, and trading
involves risk of loss. The app is independent and not affiliated with NSE or
Zerodha.
